Paginae is your personal reading companion.

BUILD YOUR LIBRARY
Add books by searching Open Library, scanning a barcode, or entering them manually. Assign covers, formats, and reading statuses to keep everything in order.

TRACK EVERY SESSION
Start the reading timer whenever you sit down with a book. Log your progress to track your reading history.

JOURNAL YOUR READING
Browse the calendar to see when and what you read. Tap any day to revisit your sessions, see progress made, and reflect on your reading habits.

TAKE NOTES
Capture and share thoughts, quotes, and reminders for any book.

Paginae respects your privacy — everything stays on your device.
